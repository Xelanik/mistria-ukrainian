[b][size=5]English[/size][/b]
Unofficial Ukrainian localization for [b]Fields of Mistria[/b], installed via MOMI. Version {{version}} translates the [b]UI[/b] (menus, settings, tutorials and tutorial cards, HUD, crafting, customization, skills, skill perks, festivals, locations, NPC names and jobs, dates, infusions, bells, animals), [b]all items[/b] (names and descriptions), quests and the Request Board, 206 letters, clothing, animals and Museum sets. Cutscenes and dialogue follow in later versions; anything untranslated stays in English.

[b]Install:[/b] update MOMI to the latest version, put the [b]FoM-UA[/b] folder into [b]mods/[/b], enable it in MOMI and press Apply, then pick Ukrainian in the in-game language menu. The startup loading bird stays in English. Do not enable it together with another mod that adds the [i]ukr[/i] language.

Draft translations were produced with AI assistance (Claude) and have not been fully proofread yet. Translation: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, code: MIT. Not affiliated with NPC Studio.
